A Fully Automated Integrated Water Monitoring Platform Based on 5G and AI with Edge-to-Cloud Collaboration

By integrating 5G technology with multiple deep learning algorithms and an edge-to-cloud collaborative framework, the system enables precise monitoring of water quality and underwater organisms, route planning, and autonomous navigation. Five field deployments had been completed by July 2025.
